# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email the address on the site's [Security page](https://argonite.org/legal/security) (`security@` the site's domain). Please don't open a public issue for vulnerabilities.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and what an attacker could do with it. We'll reply within a few days and keep you posted until it's fixed. We don't take legal action against good-faith research that avoids privacy violations, data destruction and service disruption.

To report a **malicious project or file**, use the report button on its page instead. Moderators can revoke a version instantly.

## Out of scope

Scanning lowers risk but can't prove a file is safe. Files downloaded directly from GitHub, outside the site, aren't verified.
